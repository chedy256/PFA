Privacy Policy for ISIMM Intern

Effective Date: February 15, 2026

1. Introduction

ISIMM Intern ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile and web application.

2. Information We Collect

We strictly limit data collection to what is necessary for the internship management process:

  • Google User Data: When you sign in with Google, we collect your email address (`@isimm.u-monastir.tn` or similar), your name, and your profile picture to identify you within the system.
  • Academic Data: Internship requests, reports, and evaluations you submit or receive.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for:

  • Authenticating your identity as a student or faculty member.
  • Managing internship workflows (validations, assignments, grading).
  • Generating official academic documents (certificates, internship agreements).

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. Your data is effectively visible only to:

  • University Administration: For validation and archival purposes.
  • Supervising Teachers: To evaluate your performance.
